## ⚖️ Decision-Making Process

### Primary Method: 

**Option A: Consensus** – Everyone must agree before moving forward  
> ⏳ If no consensus within 45 minutes → escalate to vote

**Option B: Majority Vote** – Majority wins; minority records dissent  
> ✅ Quorum: At least 3 members present

**Option C: Role-Based Authority** – Final say based on area  
- Tech → Tech Lead  
- Research → Discovery Lead  
- Process → Program Lead  
- Strategy → Team vote  

**Option D: [Custom Method]**

### Escalation Path
1. Sleep on it → revisit next day  
2. Seek advice from another team  
3. Escalate to instructor

## ⚔️ Conflict Resolution

### Process
1. **Direct Talk (24–48h):** Private, respectful discussion  
2. **Mediation:** Neutral team member facilitates if Step 1 fails  
3. **Escalate:** Instructor mediation if still unresolved  

### Handling Common Issues

**Missed Deadlines:**  
→ Explain reason → Redistribute work → Adjust future planning  

**Unequal Contribution:**  
→ Address privately → Discuss at meeting → Adjust workloads  

**Technical Disagreements:**  
→ Tech Lead researches → Team discusses → Vote if needed  

**Personal Conflicts:**  
→ Use 3-step process above; prioritize respect
