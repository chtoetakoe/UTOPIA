## Final Problem Statement

**Problem:**  
Many KIU students struggle to maintain a healthy balance between academic responsibilities and their mental well-being. The constant workload, long hours of study, and pressure to perform often lead to burnout, anxiety, and emotional exhaustion. Most students keep these feelings to themselves, as open discussions about mental health are still uncommon in academic settings.

**Who Experiences It:**  
KIU students from all study years — especially those managing multiple deadlines, living away from family, or juggling part-time jobs and coursework.

**When / Where It Happens:**

- During exam and project weeks, when workload peaks
- Late at night, when students feel isolated or overwhelmed
- Throughout the semester, as academic stress slowly builds

**Why It Matters:**  
Poor mental health directly impacts learning, focus, motivation, and long-term success. A solution that supports emotional awareness and community connection could help students feel less alone, more supported, and more productive.

## Why This Problem Matters Most

Unlike minor inconveniences (e.g., event coordination or study group scheduling), this problem affects **nearly every KIU student** at a personal level.  
It impacts academic performance, motivation, and retention.  
Addressing mental well-being is not only about reducing stress — it’s about helping students **thrive emotionally and academically.**

**Team UTOPIA’s Goal:**  
To design a digital solution that helps students reflect on their emotional state, connect with peers who understand their experiences, and find balance through shared support and mindful practices.
