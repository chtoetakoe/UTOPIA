## Problem Deep Dive

### The Story: What Happened

Student-Epsilon shared that he failed the Databases course last semester after submitting the final project incomplete. He said, “I froze — I couldn’t even open pgAdmin the last week.” He blamed anxiety and burnout, not lack of ability.

This semester he’s retaking the course, but anxiety has intensified. Every lecture reminder “feels like a flashback.” He described sitting in class physically tense, afraid of being called to answer. “I know the material, but I panic that I’ll blank out.”

He studies alone because his project partners from last year “moved on.” Friends are supportive. He hides his fear of failing again, fearing it would confirm he’s not capable. He said, “If I fail again, I have to leave KIU. I try to study, but sometimes I just stare at the screen until 2 a.m.”

He wants to talk about it but feels that other students “don’t really understand.” Professors are kind but “too busy to talk.” He sometimes walks around the dorm at night to calm down. He said the worst part isn’t studying, but “the silence and overthinking after midnight.”

---

### Five Whys / Root Cause Exploration

**Surface problem:** Severe anxiety and pressure related to retaking Databases.  
**Why #1:** Why does this course cause so much stress? → “It’s the only one I failed — it feels like a symbol of failure.”  
**Why #2:** Why does that symbol matter so much? → “If I fail again, I can’t stay here. It’s like the end.”  
**Why #3:** Why not talk to anyone about it? → “I don’t want pity or judgment.”  
**Why #4:** Why afraid of judgment? → “Because people expect me to be doing well. I’m supposed to be the smart one.”  
**Root cause:** Fear of failure combined with shame and lack of safe emotional outlet — internalized pressure to maintain image of competence.
