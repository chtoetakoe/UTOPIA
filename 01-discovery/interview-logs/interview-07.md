## Problem Deep Dive  

**The Story: What Happened**  
Student-Eta described his typical morning routine: alarm goes off at 7 AM, he rushes to get ready, grabs a quick breakfast, and leaves by 8:10 to catch the marshrutka. “Some mornings the marshrutka is packed and I have to wait for the next one,” he said. “That means I’m late, and professors notice.”  

He recalled missing the marshrutka during midterms and having to take a taxi: “I spent 15 lari just to not miss an exam. That’s like three days of lunch money.”  

Living at home wasn’t his choice — dorms were full when he applied. “I thought it would be fine,” he said. “But by third year, watching my friends hang out after class, going to study sessions at 8 PM, staying up coding together… I just go home and crash.”  

Now in 7th semester, every assignment feels critical. “I check Moodle constantly,” he admitted. “Even when I know nothing’s posted yet. It’s like I’m waiting for something to go wrong.”  

He’s passing all courses but feels trapped by fear. “If I fail one course, I don’t graduate. My parents have already planned around me graduating this year.”  

Isolation hits hardest during group projects: “Everyone wants to meet at the dorms or study late on campus, but I have to catch the last marshrutka by 10. So I end up doing my part alone at home, and I feel disconnected.”  

When asked what would help, he replied: “Honestly? Just don’t schedule core CS courses at 9 AM. Or give us some online options so I don’t have to physically be there for everything. I’m exhausted before the semester even starts.”

## Five Whys / Root Cause Exploration  

- **Surface problem:** Severe exhaustion and anxiety from commuting to early lectures.  
- **Why #1:** Why is commuting so draining? → “Because I lose 2–3 hours every day just traveling, and I’m already tired before class starts.”  
- **Why #2:** Why does that create such anxiety? → “Because if I’m late or miss attendance, it affects my grade, and I can’t afford any mistakes.”  
- **Why #3:** Why couldn’t he get dorm housing? → “Dorms were full when I applied, and even now there’s a waiting list.”  
- **Why #4:** Why does this feel unbearable specifically now? → “Because this is my last semester — if I fail anything, I have to stay another whole year.”  
- **Root cause:** Limited housing capacity and inflexible early-morning scheduling create structural disadvantages for commuting students, amplified by high-stakes graduation requirements.
