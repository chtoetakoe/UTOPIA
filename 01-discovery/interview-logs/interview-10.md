## Problem Deep Dive

**The Story: What Happened**
Student-Mu failed Data Structures in second year, learning later about the **fail twice, automatic expulsion** policy. She has spent over a year in constant anxiety, hiding the situation from her family who are unaware she failed the first time. The fear prevents her from concentrating, even though she is studying the retake course **6-8 hours daily**—her brain "**just stops working when I'm that anxious**."

The preoccupation with Data Structures has led to her neglecting other important courses (Operating Systems, Software Engineering), where her grades are now slipping. She tells herself, "**Just survive Data Structures, nothing else matters**," but recognizes that everything else does matter.

The pressure has caused severe physical and emotional distress, including **insomnia**, avoiding calls with parents, and social withdrawal ("I can't pretend to be normal"). She tearfully described scenarios where she imagines seeing the final grade and having to face her parents, concluding, "**Sometimes I imagine just disappearing, not telling anyone**." She has not sought counseling or advising, believing they would simply tell her to "**Try harder?**"

---

## Five Whys / Root Cause Exploration

- **Surface problem:** Extreme stress and inability to concentrate on other courses while retaking failed course.
- **Why #1:** Why is retaking so stressful? → "Because if I fail again, I get expelled. There's no third chance."
- **Why #2:** Why does that fear affect other courses? → "Because I can't think about anything except not failing Data Structures. Everything else feels less important."
- **Why #3:** Why can't she focus despite knowing other courses matter? → "Because the fear is **paralyzing**. My brain just keeps playing worst-case scenarios."
- **Why #4:** Why hasn't she sought help or told anyone? → "Because I'm **ashamed**, and I'm scared of making it real. And I don't think anyone can actually help."
- **Root cause:** **High-stakes expulsion policy** creates paralyzing fear that prevents effective studying and causes **cascading academic failure** across all courses, while shame and stigma around failure prevent students from seeking support.
