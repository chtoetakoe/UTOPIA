## I. North Star Metric & Activation Flow

This section defines the key metrics that will guide the product development following the successful validation of initial demand.

### 🌟 North Star Metric (NSM)

| Metric | Definition | Why This Metric | Target |

| **Frequency of Meaningful Engagement (FME)** | The percentage of *Active Users* who complete a check-in **AND** consume the subsequent peer support content or reply (their "aha moment") **3 or more times per week**. | This captures the core value delivered: users inputting their pain (Check-in) and receiving emotional relief/validation (Consumption). | **50% of Active Users** achieve FME $\ge 3\times/\text{week}$ |

## II. Validation Sprint Plan: Experiment 2 (Wizard of Oz)

**Sprint Duration:** 2 Weeks (Focus: Week 1 - Experiment 2; Week 2 - MVP Build Start)
**Sprint Goal:** **Validate Efficacy**—prove that *peer-based support replies* (our proposed solution) actually make users feel better.

### What We're Testing (Experiment 2)

| Component | Detail | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hypothesis** | Peer support replies (manual, supportive, and anonymous) will be rated as $\ge 4/5$ helpful. | `uploaded:experiment-plan.md` |
| **Riskiest Assumption** | That peer-driven content provides the necessary feeling of validation and belonging. | `uploaded:experiment-plan.md` |
| **Method** | **Wizard of Oz:** Students submit a "Stress Vent" via form, and team members manually write supportive, anonymous replies via email/DM. | `uploaded:experiment-plan.md` |
| **Success Criteria** | User replies to the email/DM thanking us or rating the helpfulness **$> 4/5$**. | `uploaded:experiment-plan.md` |

## III. Decision Framework & Notes

Based on the combined results of the Smoke Test (Exp 1) and the Wizard of Oz (Exp 2):

| Outcome | Interpretation | Next Steps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exp 2 VALIDATED** | Demand is proven (Exp 1) and core solution **efficacy is proven** (Exp 2). | **PERSEVERE:** Dedicate full resources to **MVP development (Sprint 1)**: Automated check-in, real-time peer reflection display, and simple nudge/link. |
| **Exp 2 INVALIDATED** | Demand is proven, but peer support **doesn't help** (e.g., users prefer institutional resources). | **ITERATE/PIVOT:** Maintain anonymous check-in but pivot the "reward" or response. Test new value prop (e.g., automated gratitude journaling, links to specific professor office hours). |
