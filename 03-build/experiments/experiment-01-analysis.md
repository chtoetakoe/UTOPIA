## Key Insights

### 1. The Problem is Urgent ("Hair on Fire")
Our data shows that **66% of students** are experiencing stress multiple times a week or daily. This confirms the intensity of the problem is high (Level 5/5), justifying an immediate solution.

### 2. Solution Refinement: Connection > Venting
We assumed "Anonymous Venting" would be the main draw. However, **50% of users voted for a "Relatability Feed"** (seeing that others are stressed about the same exam), while only 33% voted for Venting.
*   **Implication:** Users want validation ("I am not alone") more than just shouting into the void. We should prioritize the Feed feature.

### 3. Mobile Web is the Way
56.7% of users requested a **Mobile Website** (No download needed). This validates our technical decision to use React/Vite for a PWA rather than building a native Flutter/iOS app immediately.

## Comparison to Assumptions
- **VALIDATED:** Students are willing to share emails for this solution.
- **VALIDATED:** Social stigma is a barrier (Question 5 in survey confirmed fears of looking weak).
- **INVALIDATED:** The assumption that "Venting" is the #1 feature. "Relatability" is #1.
