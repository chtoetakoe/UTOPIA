# Experiment 01 Analysis

## Results vs Success Criteria
| Metric | Target | Actual | Result |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Conversion Rate | 15% | 18.3% | ✅ Met |
| Total Signups | 50 | 26 | ❌ Missed (Traffic low) |

## Analysis
**Why we missed total signups:** We underestimated the difficulty of driving traffic during midterms. However, the *conversion rate* (people who saw it vs. signed up) was very high (18.3%), indicating strong product-market fit for those who did see it.

**Key Insight:** Mobile users converted at 25%, while desktop users converted at 10%. This validates our assumption that the problem is experienced "on the go."

**Comparison to Assumptions:**
- VALIDATED: Students care enough to give an email address.
- VALIDATED: The problem is acute (painful enough to act).
- UNCERTAIN: Can we acquire users cheaply at scale? (Traffic volume was low).
