# Experiment Plan

**Team:** UTOPIA
**Sprint:** Validation (Week 8)

---

## Experiment #1: Smoke Test (Landing Page)

**Type:** Landing Page with Waitlist
**Goal:** Validate demand for "Anonymous Peer Support" before building the full backend.

**What We Will Build:**
- A simple Carrd.co or Notion page.
- Headline: "Venting about finals shouldn't feel risky."
- Value Prop: "Anonymous. Safe. Students only."
- CTA: "Join the Beta Waitlist."

**Metrics:**
- **Primary:** Conversion Rate (Signups / Unique Visitors).
- **Target:** >15%.

**Distribution:**
- Share in 3 KIU WhatsApp/Telegram groups.
- Put up 5 flyers with QR codes in the library.

---

## Experiment #2: Wizard of Oz (Manual Support)

**Type:** Concierge / Wizard of Oz
**Goal:** Test if "replies" actually make people feel better.

**Setup:**
- Create a Google Form where students can submit a "Stress Vent".
- Team members (acting as the system) manually write supportive, anonymous replies via email within 2 hours.

**Success Criteria:**
- User replies to the email thanking us or rating the helpfulness > 4/5.

---

## Decision Tree
- **If Exp #1 > 15%:** Proceed to build MVP (Sprint 1 Plan).
- **If Exp #1 < 5%:** Re-interview students to find better value proposition wording.
