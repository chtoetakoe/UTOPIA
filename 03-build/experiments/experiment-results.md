# Experiment Results Tracker

**Experiment:** Smoke Test #1 (Waitlist Signups)
**Hypothesis:** Students will sign up for an anonymous support tool to avoid judgment.
**Success Metric:** >15% Conversion Rate (Signups / Visitors).
**Date Range:** Nov 14 - Nov 17

---

## 📊 Daily Metrics

| Date | Visitors (Clicks) | Signups | Conv. Rate | Context / Notes |
|------|-------------------|---------|------------|-----------------|
| **Nov 14** | 92 | 19 | **20.6%** | Posted in CS WhatsApp Group & Discord. High initial interest. |
| **Nov 15** | 45 | 5 | **11.1%** | Traffic slowed down. Posted QR codes in library. |
| **Nov 16** | 28 | 4 | **14.2%** | Weekend traffic (mostly library scanners). |
| **TOTAL** | **165** | **28** | **16.9%** | **Target Met (16.9% > 15%)** |

## 💬 Qualitative Feedback
*Comments collected via DM or the "Why are you interested?" field in the form.*

**Positive Signals:**
- "I really need this for finals week. Everyone acts like they are fine but I know they aren't."
- "As long as it's actually anonymous, I'm in."
- "Can we talk about bad professors here too?"

**Concerns/Questions:**
- "Will the university see my data?" (Privacy concern)
- "Is this just for CS majors?" (Scope question)

---

## 🧠 Analysis & Learnings

**What worked:**
- The headline "Drowning in deadlines?" resonated well. High click-through rate from WhatsApp.
- Privacy/Anonymity is definitely the main selling point.

**What didn't work:**
- Library QR codes had low scans compared to digital links (too much friction to scan while walking).
