# Product Hypothesis

**Team:** UTOPIA
**Date:** 2025-11-14

---

## Core Hypothesis
We believe that **KIU students feeling academic stress** will **sign up for an anonymous support tool** because **they currently fear judgment when venting on public social media**.

## Measurable Outcome
We will know we are right when we achieve **15% conversion rate** on our landing page (Signups / Visitors).

## Underlying Assumptions
1. Students trust us enough to give their email (even for an "anonymous" tool).
2. The promise of "anonymity" is the primary driver for signups.
3. Students identify with the phrase "academic burnout."
