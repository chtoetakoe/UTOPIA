## 🎬 Demo Story

### The User

**User Persona:**

- Name: Ramazi (fictional)
- Background: 2nd-year KIU student
- Current situation: Studying alone late at night during midterm week
- Goal: Feel less alone and emotionally supported without being judged

**Why This Persona?**

```
This persona directly reflects our validated ICP.
8 out of 10 interviewed students described late-night stress and isolation.
Ramazi represents both the emotional and contextual peak of the problem.
```

---

### Story Arc

**SETUP (10 seconds)**

What you'll say:

```
"Meet Ramazi, a second-year KIU student. It's 11:30 PM, midterm week.
he's overwhelmed, stressed, and studying alone in his dorm room."
```

What's on screen:

```
Home Feed screen of UTOPIA with calm UI and anonymous posts visible.
```

---

**CHALLENGE (5 seconds)**

What you'll say:

```
"Like many students, Ramazi feels like he's the only one struggling —
and he doesn't want to admit it to anyone."
```

What's on screen:

```
Scrolling through anonymous posts showing similar stress experiences.
```

---

**SOLUTION (45–55 seconds)**

What you'll say:

```
"Ramazi opens UTOPIA — a safe, anonymous space for students to share
and validate each other's stress without fear of judgment.

he reads posts from other KIU students going through the same thing.
he realizes he's not alone.

Ramazi writes a short post about how overwhelmed he feels.
No login. No profile. Just honesty.

Within seconds, other students react with 'I feel this too'.
That simple validation helps break the feeling of isolation."
```

What's on screen:

```
- Scroll through feed
- Tap “New Post”
- Type short post
- Submit
- Return to feed
- Reaction counter increasing
```

---

**RESOLUTION (5–10 seconds)**

What you'll say:

```
"UTOPIA doesn’t solve exams —
it solves the feeling of being alone while facing them."
```

What's on screen:

```
Final calm feed view with multiple posts and reactions visible.
```
