## Slide 2: Problem

**Target Customer (WHO):**
First–third year KIU students taking 5+ courses during high-pressure academic periods.

**The Problem (WHAT):**
Students struggle with the **emotional toll of academic pressure**, not because help doesn’t exist, but because **admitting stress feels like failure**.

**Quantified Impact:**

- 9/10 interviews: fear of judgment
- 8/10 interviews: feeling isolated
- 7/10 interviews: burnout cycles

**Why Existing Solutions Fail:**

- Counseling feels too formal
- Friends appear “fine” → comparison
- No safe, informal peer validation

**Evidence from Customer Discovery:**

- Interviews: 10
- % experiencing isolation: 80%
- Quote: “I feel like I’m the only one struggling.”

## Slide 3: Solution

**Solution Statement:**
UTOPIA is a private, low-effort peer space where students can feel validated without fear of judgment.

**How It Works (3 steps):**

1. Student checks in anonymously
2. Sees others struggling with similar pressures
3. Receives validation, not advice or diagnosis

**Why It’s Different:**

- No performance culture
- No fixing, only understanding
- Built for late-night isolation moments

**Key Outcome:**
Students feel less alone and avoid unhealthy coping cycles.

**Visuals Needed:**

- Simple flow diagram
- “Before / After” emotional state
