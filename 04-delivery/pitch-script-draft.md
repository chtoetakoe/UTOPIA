# Pitch Script — Demo Day

**Product Name:** UTOPIA

## PART 1: HOOK & PROBLEM [0:00–1:30]

**Speaker:** Teona

---

**[SLIDE 1: Title / Hook]** `[0:00]`

**Opening Line (Hook):**

> “Nine out of ten KIU students are afraid to admit they’re struggling — even when they’re burning out.”

[PAUSE]

> “Not because help doesn’t exist.
> But because admitting stress feels like admitting weakness.”

`[0:15]` **Introduction:**

> “Hi, I’m Teona, and together with Ani, Saba, and Aleksi, we’re UTOPIA.
> We’re building for a problem we live through ourselves.”

---

**[SLIDE 2: Problem]** `[0:30]`

**Problem Statement:**

> “Here’s the problem.
> First- to third-year KIU students with heavy course loads struggle with the emotional toll of academic pressure — especially during midterms and finals.”

[PAUSE]

> “But the real issue isn’t stress itself.
> It’s isolation.”

> “In our interviews, students described studying alone late at night — between 9 PM and 1 AM — feeling like they were the only ones failing, while everyone else seemed fine.”

**Supporting Evidence:**

> “We interviewed 10 students.
> • 9 out of 10 feared being judged if they admitted stress.
> • 8 out of 10 felt intensely alone.
> • 7 out of 10 described repeating burnout cycles.”

> “One student told us:
> ‘I passed the exam, but I didn’t actually learn anything — I was just exhausted.’”

`[1:15]` **Transition to Solution:**

> “That’s why we didn’t build another productivity tool.
> We built UTOPIA.”

---

## PART 2: SOLUTION [1:30–2:30]

**Speaker:** Teona

---

**[SLIDE 3: Solution]** `[1:30]`

**Solution Statement:**

> “UTOPIA is a private, stigma-free peer space where students can feel understood — without having to explain themselves.”

> “It’s not about advice.
> It’s not about fixing people.
> It’s about validation.”

**How It Works:**

> “Here’s how it works:
> First, a student checks in anonymously during a high-pressure moment.
> Second, they immediately see others experiencing the same emotions.
> Third, they receive simple peer validation — not judgment, not comparison.”

> “The outcome is simple but powerful:
> students stop feeling alone — and stop spiraling into unhealthy coping.”

`[2:15]` **Transition to Product:**

> “Let me show you what that looks like.”
