# Unit Economics Analysis

**Team Name:** UTOPIA

**Product:** Peer Support Platform for Students
**Date:** December 11, 2025

---

## 📊 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

### Sales & Marketing Spend Breakdown (Projected Month 1)

**Strategy:** Viral campus marketing (flyers/stickers) + Targeted Social Ads.

- **Ad spend:** $300 (Instagram/TikTok targeting KIU geolocation)
- **Marketing tools:** $30 (Canva Pro, Linktree)
- **Content creation:** $70 (Printing physical flyers/QR stickers for library)
- **Sales team time:** $0 (Founders doing outreach)
- **Total S&M Spend:** **$400**

**New Customers Acquired:** 50
*(Based on converting 28 waitlist users + 22 new organic signups from launch)*

**CAC Calculation:**
$$ CAC = \frac{\$400}{50} = \$8.00 \text{ per customer} $$

---

## 💰 Lifetime Value (LTV)

### Method: Freemium Subscription (B2C)
*Assumption: Basic peer support is free. Premium tier ($4.99/mo) offers mood analytics history and priority matching.*

**Formula:** $LTV = ARPU \times Gross Margin \% \times (\frac{1}{Churn Rate})$

- **ARPU (Avg Revenue Per User):** $4.99
- **Gross Margin %:** 85%
  - *(Software product; main cost is hosting/DB which is low per user)*
- **Monthly Churn Rate:** 8%
  - *(Higher churn expected due to semester breaks/summer holidays)*
- **Customer Lifespan:** 12.5 months

**LTV Calculation:**
$$ LTV = \$4.99 \times 0.85 \times (\frac{1}{0.08}) $$
$$ LTV = \$4.24 \times 12.5 $$
$$ LTV \approx \$53.00 $$

---

## 📈 Key Metrics Analysis

### 1. LTV:CAC Ratio
$$ Ratio = \frac{\$53.00}{\$8.00} = 6.6:1 $$

**Status:** ✅ **Excellent (>5:1)**
*Analysis: Our ratio is high because our acquisition strategy relies heavily on low-cost, organic campus growth (word-of-mouth, physical stickers) rather than expensive paid ads.*

### 2. Payback Period
$$ Payback = \frac{\$8.00}{\$4.24 \text{ (Margin Contribution)}} \approx 1.9 \text{ months} $$

**Status:** ✅ **Excellent (<6 months)**
*Analysis: We recover our marketing spend very quickly, allowing us to reinvest in growth efficiently.*

### 3. Gross Margin
**Value:** 85%
**Status:** ✅ **Good (>80%)**
*Analysis: Standard for lightweight software SaaS.*

---

## 🎯 Business Model Health Assessment

**Overall Health:** ☑ Strong | ☐ Moderate | ☐ Needs Work

**Strengths:**
1. **Viral Coefficient:** University campuses are dense networks; word-of-mouth lowers CAC significantly.
2. **Efficiency:** <2 month payback period is extremely healthy for a startup.

**Risks & Mitigations:**
1. **Churn Risks:** Students may delete the app during summer break.
   *Mitigation:* Introduce "off-semester" content or "pause subscription" features.
2. **Monetization:** Converting free users to paid is the biggest challenge.
   *Mitigation:* Keep the paywall low ($4.99) and focus on high-value analytics features.
