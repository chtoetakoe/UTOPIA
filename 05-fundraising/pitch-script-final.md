PART 1: HOOK & PROBLEM [0:00–1:30]

Speaker: Teona

[SLIDE 1: Title / Hook] [0:00]
“Nine out of ten KIU students are afraid to admit they’re struggling — even when they’re burning out. [PAUSE] Not because help doesn’t exist. But because admitting stress feels like admitting weakness. Hi, I’m Teona, and together with Ani, Saba, and Aleksi, we’re UTOPIA. We’re building for a problem we live through ourselves.”

[SLIDE 2: Problem] [0:30]
“Here’s the problem. First- to third-year KIU students with heavy course loads struggle with the emotional toll of academic pressure. But the real issue isn't the workload. It’s isolation. In our interviews, students described studying alone at 11:00 PM, feeling like they were the only ones failing, while everyone else seemed fine. We interviewed 10 students: 9 out of 10 fear judgment, 80% feel intensely alone, and 70% experience a 'burnout crash' after every exam cycle. One student, Nino, told us: ‘At night in my room, I feel like I'm the only person in the world who doesn't get it.’ We didn't build another productivity tool. We built a validation engine.”

PART 2: SOLUTION [1:30–2:30]

Speaker: Teona

[SLIDE 3: Solution] [1:30]
“UTOPIA is a private, stigma-free peer space where students feel understood without having to explain themselves. It’s not about fixing people; it’s about validation. First, a student checks in anonymously during a high-pressure moment. Second, they immediately see others experiencing the same emotions in a relatability feed. Third, they receive simple peer validation — a heart, a 'me too,' or a supportive seed. The outcome? The isolation loop is broken before it leads to burnout.”

PART 4: MARKET & BUSINESS [4:00–5:15]

Speaker: Ani

[SLIDE 5: Why Now?] [4:00]
“Existing tools are too formal or have the wrong focus. Counseling is for crises; LMS is for grades. There is zero space for informal, student-to-student support. UTOPIA fills that gap.”

[SLIDE 6: Market Opportunity] [4:30]
“Academic pressure is a global epidemic. In Georgia alone, we have a 12.5 million dollar total addressable market. We are starting with our beachhead: the 1,500 students at KIU, a high-pressure STEM environment where our validation is strongest.”

[SLIDE 7: Business Model] [5:00]
“We use a freemium model. Core support is free, while premium features like mood analytics cost $4.99. Our unit economics are highly efficient: an 8-dollar CAC and a 53-dollar LTV, giving us a 6.6-to-1 ratio. We pay back our acquisition costs in less than two months.”
