# Comparable Companies Analysis

**Team Name:** UTOPIA

**Sector:** Mental Health / EdTech

**Analysis Date:** December 11, 2025

---

## 🔍 Selection Criteria

We selected companies that operate at the intersection of **Mental Health**, **Community/Peer Support**, and *
*Subscription Models**.

---

## 📊 Comparable Company #1: Togetherall

* **What They Do:** An anonymous, peer-to-peer mental health support community (direct competitor model).
* **Business Model:** B2B2C (Sells to Universities/Healthcare).
* **Valuation Indicators:**
    * Acquired by FTV Capital (Private Equity).
    * Typically trades at **6x - 8x Revenue** (SaaS/Healthcare standard).
* **Comparability:** **High**. Their core product (anonymous peer support) is identical to ours.

## 📊 Comparable Company #2: Headspace Health (formerly Ginger)

* **What They Do:** Mental healthcare app combining coaching and self-care.
* **Business Model:** Subscription ($12.99/mo) + Enterprise.
* **Valuation Indicators:**
    * Valuation: ~$3 Billion (2021).
    * Revenue Multiple: **~8x - 10x** (Premium Wellness Brand).
* **Comparability:** **Medium**. They are content-heavy; we are community-heavy.

## 📊 Comparable Company #3: Talkspace

* **What They Do:** Online therapy platform connecting users to licensed professionals.
* **Business Model:** Marketplace/Service.
* **Valuation Indicators:**
    * Publicly Traded (NASDAQ: TALK).
    * Revenue Multiple: **~2.5x**.
* **Comparability:** **Low**. Their margins are low because they pay expensive therapists. Our software-only margins (
  85%) justify a higher multiple.

---

## 🧮 Selected Exit Multiple: 7x

**Justification:** We command a higher multiple than service-heavy companies (Talkspace) due to our high margins, but
slightly lower than established brands (Headspace) due to our early stage. **7x** aligns with the **Togetherall**
peer-support SaaS model.
