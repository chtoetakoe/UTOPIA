**Comparable Companies Analysis – UTOPIA**

**Team Name:** UTOPIA
**Product:** Anonymous Mood Tracking & Emotional Support App for KIU Students
**Analysis Date:** December 2025

---

# 🎯 Purpose

To benchmark UTOPIA’s valuation using revenue multiples from comparable mental-health and emotional-support apps with similar business models, user bases, and product experiences.

---

# 🔍 Selection Criteria

**Comparable companies must match UTOPIA in:**

- **Industry:** Mental health / emotional wellbeing / digital therapeutics
- **Business model:** Freemium + subscription (premium emotional support features)
- **Customer segment:** Students, Gen-Z, mental-health-aware users
- **Stage:** Early Seed – Series A
- **Product type:** AI-driven or prompt-driven emotional support
- **Geography:** Global (but B2C, lightweight apps)

**Why these criteria?**
UTOPIA is a **consumer mental-health product** targeted at students. Comparable companies must operate in a **similar market**, with **similar monetization**, and be at a **similar maturity stage** to predict realistic multiples.

---

# 📊 Comparable Company #1 — **Wysa**

### Company Overview

**Company Name:** Wysa
**Website:** [https://www.wysa.com/](https://www.wysa.com/)
**Founded:** 2015
**Location:** Boston / Bangalore

**What They Do:**
AI-driven mental health chatbot providing CBT exercises and emotional support.

**Business Model:**

- Revenue model: **Freemium + Subscription ($9.99/month)**
- Customer segment: B2C + B2B (schools, employers)

---

### Financial Data

**Most Recent Funding Round:**

- Date: 2021
- Round: **Series B**
- Amount raised: **$20M**
- Valuation: **~$60M** (industry estimate)
- Lead investor(s): Healthquad, British International Investment

**Revenue at time of funding:**

- Annual revenue (estimated): **$15M**
- Growth rate: **~45% YoY**

**Key Metrics:**

- Customers: 4M+
- ARR: ~$15M
- Team size: ~100

---

### Valuation Analysis

```
Revenue Multiple = Valuation / Annual Revenue
Revenue Multiple = $60M / $15M
Revenue Multiple = 4.0x
```

**Comparability:** ☑ HIGH
**Why:** AI emotional-support app, subscription model, similar UX.

**Adjustments:** Larger team + older market presence → use carefully but still highly relevant.
