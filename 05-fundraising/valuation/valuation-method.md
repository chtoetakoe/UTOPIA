## 🎯 VC Method Calculation

### Step 1: Project Year 5 Revenue

**Assumption:** By Year 5, UTOPIA expands from KIU to **50 Universities** in the region.

* **Target:** 50 Schools × 1,000 Paid Users/School × $120/yr ($10/mo avg blended B2B/B2C)
* **Year 5 Revenue:** **$6,000,000**

### Step 2: Determine Exit Value

* **Selected Multiple:** **7x** (Based on Comps like Togetherall)
* **Terminal Value:** $6M × 7 = **$42,000,000**

### Step 3: Calculate Pre-Money Valuation

* **Target Investor ROI:** **20x** (Standard for Pre-Seed/Seed risk profile)
* **Formula:** Terminal Value / Target ROI
* $$ \$42,000,000 / 20 = \$2,100,000 $$

**Result:** **$2.1 Million**
