# Prioritization Framework

**Product:** UTOPIA  
**Team:** UTOPIA  
**Date:** December 2025

---

## Framework Purpose

This framework defines how product decisions are prioritized to ensure alignment with UTOPIA’s core mission: reducing isolation caused by academic stress and stigma.

All prioritization decisions balance emotional impact, user safety, and feasibility.

---

## Primary Framework Used: RICE (Adapted)

We use a modified **RICE** framework with an added **Safety Constraint**.

### Scoring Dimensions

**Reach**  
How many users are affected within a semester.

**Impact**  
Degree to which the feature reduces isolation or increases validation.

**Confidence**  
Strength of evidence from interviews, experiments, or usage.

**Effort**  
Estimated development and cognitive complexity.

**Safety Check (Mandatory Gate)**  
Any feature that risks anonymity, judgment, or emotional harm is deprioritized regardless of score.

---

## Decision Rule

A feature is prioritized only if:

- It scores high on Impact
- It passes the Safety Check
- It aligns with validated user pain

---

## Example Prioritization Decisions

### High Priority

- Commenting with supportive constraints
- Improved feed relevance
- Reaction system refinements

**Reason:** Directly strengthens validation loop with low risk.

---

### Medium Priority

- Post categorization
- Similar-post discovery

**Reason:** Improves engagement but not core validation.

---

### Low Priority / Deferred

- Profiles or identities
- Direct messaging
- Gamification

**Reason:** Increases risk of judgment, comparison, and misuse.

---

## Trade-offs Accepted

- Slower growth in exchange for trust
- Limited features in exchange for clarity
- Manual moderation strategies over automation

---

## Review Process

Prioritization is reviewed:

- After each validation phase
- When new evidence emerges
- Before any expansion decision

Decisions are documented and reversible.

---

## Final Principle

In UTOPIA, **what we don’t build is as important as what we build**.

Every feature must earn its place through evidence and alignment with emotional safety.
