# Product Roadmap

**Product:** UTOPIA  
**Team:** UTOPIA  
**Time Horizon:** January 2026 – June 2026

---

## Roadmap Purpose

This roadmap outlines the strategic evolution of UTOPIA following Demo Day.  
It prioritizes validation, trust, and safety before scale, ensuring that future development aligns with the core problem of stigma-driven isolation among students.

The roadmap is intentionally conservative and evidence-driven.

---

## Phase 1: Post-Demo Stabilization (Month 1)

**Goals:**

- Solidify MVP experience
- Ensure emotional safety and usability
- Prepare for controlled pilot use

**Key Initiatives:**

- UI polish and accessibility improvements
- Content clarity and tone refinement
- Bug fixes and performance optimization
- Clear safety messaging and boundaries

**Success Criteria:**

- Smooth posting and reacting flow
- No critical usability issues
- Positive qualitative feedback from pilot users

---

## Phase 2: Validation & Retention (Months 2–3)

**Goals:**

- Increase repeat usage
- Strengthen emotional validation loop
- Measure early retention signals

**Key Initiatives:**

- Lightweight commenting on posts
- Improved feed relevance (recency + resonance)
- Reaction analytics (aggregate only, anonymous)
- Clear community norms reinforcement

**Success Criteria:**

- Users return voluntarily
- Increased reactions per post
- Reduced drop-off after first use

---

## Phase 3: Trust & Community Signals (Months 4–5)

**Goals:**

- Deepen sense of shared experience
- Maintain anonymity while improving connection

**Key Initiatives:**

- “Similar posts” discovery
- Thematic clustering of posts (e.g., exams, major choice)
- Time-based prompts during high-stress periods

**Success Criteria:**

- Longer session duration
- More engagement per visit
- Strong qualitative feedback on “feeling understood”

---

## Phase 4: Institutional Expansion (Month 6)

**Goals:**

- Prepare for expansion beyond KIU
- Explore partnerships without breaking trust

**Key Initiatives:**

- University-level deployment guidelines
- Ethical review of data handling
- Optional institutional landing pages (read-only insights)

**Success Criteria:**

- Interest from additional universities
- No compromise of anonymity or safety principles

---

## Strategic Principles

- Emotional safety over growth
- Anonymity as a non-negotiable constraint
- Validation before monetization
- Evidence before expansion

---

## Roadmap Ownership

This roadmap will be revisited quarterly and adjusted based on:

- User feedback
- Retention data
- Safety considerations
- Institutional context
