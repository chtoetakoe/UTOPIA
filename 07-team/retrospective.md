# Team Retrospective

**Team Name:** UTOPIA  
**Course:** Product Development for Software Engineers  
**Date:** December 2025

---

## What Went Well

- Deep customer discovery led to a clear, validated problem
- Strong alignment on emotional safety and stigma avoidance
- Clear documentation improved decision-making
- MVP scope remained focused and realistic

---

## What Was Challenging

- Translating emotional problems into product features
- Managing time during peak academic periods
- Balancing polish vs. progress near final deadlines

---

## What We Learned

- Evidence should drive every product decision
- Saying “no” to features is as important as building
- Emotional trust is a core product constraint
- A clear story is critical for demos and pitches

---

## What We Would Do Differently

- Prototype earlier to reduce uncertainty
- Schedule more frequent short syncs
- Lock demo narrative earlier in the semester

---

## Final Reflection

This project emphasized disciplined product thinking over feature-building.  
The structured process—from discovery to demo—shaped how we approach ambiguous problems.

UTOPIA reflects not only a product outcome, but a mindset shift toward evidence-based development.
