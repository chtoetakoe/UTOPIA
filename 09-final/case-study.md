# UTOPIA – Case Study

## Overview

UTOPIA is an anonymous peer-support web application designed to reduce feelings of isolation and stigma among KIU students experiencing academic stress.

This case study documents the problem discovery, validation, design decisions, and outcomes of the project.

---

## Problem Discovery

We conducted 10 in-depth interviews with first–third year KIU students.
Key recurring patterns included fear of judgment, emotional isolation, and unhealthy coping mechanisms during exam periods.

---

## Insights & Root Cause

The core issue was not lack of productivity tools, but the absence of safe, informal peer validation.
A culture of perceived competence discouraged students from admitting vulnerability.

---

## Solution Hypothesis

We hypothesized that a lightweight, anonymous space for emotional sharing would reduce isolation and normalize stress without requiring disclosure or identity.

---

## MVP & Validation

We built an MVP using Lovable to test:

- Anonymous posting
- Peer reactions
- Low-friction emotional validation

Early validation showed strong resonance with posts and consistent peer reactions.

---

## Key Learnings

- Anonymity is essential, not optional
- Emotional validation works without advice or chat
- Simplicity builds trust

---

## What We’d Do Next

- Improve retention
- Expand safely to other universities
- Maintain strict safety constraints

---

## Reflection

UTOPIA demonstrates how product development rooted in empathy and evidence can address emotionally complex problems.
